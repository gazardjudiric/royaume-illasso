## Site rules
- Keep official information in the optional official-details module; hide absent facts instead of guessing identities or contacts.
- Use the shared event-calendar functions and the hydrated Benin calendar day for event status; centralized date logic keeps home and agenda consistent across midnight.
- Present official map assets unchanged and provide PDF and zoom through RoyalMapImage; shared rendering preserves the portrait proportions on both pages.
- Use accessible dialog components for image viewers to trap focus, restore focus and support Escape.
